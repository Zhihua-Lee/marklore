# v0.1.28 continuous jump glide with idle backfill

## Changes

Jumps smooth-scroll the full distance again (v0.1.27 cut to just short of the
target). Formulas and tables beyond the viewport render in idle time after a
note opens, in slices that yield to viewport work, jumps and active scrolling
and keep the reading line. A jump made before that completes renders the
formulas on its path first under a 120 ms budget, nearest the destination
first, and sizes only destination tables (sizing a table costs about 78 ms;
path tables keep native widths until idle). Formulas still pending while a
jump glides show as a placeholder rather than raw TeX.

Measured on a 60-section note with 300 formulas and 60 tables: backfill
completes 2.4–2.7 s after opening. Afterwards, jumps of 31k–66k px take 28–34
frames, no frame covers more than 10% of the distance, no frame shows an
unrendered formula, and headings land at 32–34 px. A jump 0.3 s after opening
starts within a frame (it previously waited about 3 s when the whole path was
prepared synchronously) and lands at 32 px.

## Automated checks

All 56 unit tests and 142 browser cases (Edge; default and reduced-motion
projects) pass. The jump regression now asserts continuity (no frame over 30%
of the distance) where smooth scrolling animates, no unrendered formula after
backfill, and no raw TeX while gliding right after opening. A new component
case checks that backfill works in small slices and waits during scrolling.

## Manual/native checks

The packaged v0.1.28 build passes all nine native suites. The smoke suite's
hover-preview step failed once when a real pointer crossed the test window
(trusted pointer events recorded) and passed on rerun. Only synthetic notes and
isolated profiles were used.

## Limits

During the first seconds after opening, a jump can pass formula placeholders
far from its destination. Physical mouse hardware was not exercised.
