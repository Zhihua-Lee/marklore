# v0.1.32 new app mark

## Changes

A new app mark in two colors only. The open book's contour forms the M, and
the handle of a one-piece mug in front of it forms a large D. Steam in the
tile color rises over the page and dissolves into the tile. Icons at 16-24 px
and the tray use the book alone (`src/folio-small.svg`), where the mug would
blur. The PNG, tray and eight-resolution ICO files are regenerated from the
SVG by `tools/build-icons.mjs`. A reading-regression helper now retries
instead of throwing when the outline is mid-rebuild.

## Automated checks

All 59 unit tests and 148 browser cases (Edge; default and reduced-motion
projects) pass.

## Manual/native checks

The icon extracted from the packaged executable is the new mark. The packaged
v0.1.32 build passes all nine native suites. On the first full run,
`native-editing.mjs` failed and passed on its own rerun. `native.mjs` failed
twice at the hover step. Recorded trusted pointer events showed the physical
mouse leaving the link, and the third run passed. Only synthetic notes and
isolated profiles were used.

## Limits

Physical mouse hardware was not exercised deliberately.
