# v0.4.1 Tab hover card; aligned task lists; direct-editing fixes

## Changes

- **Tab hover card.** Resting the pointer on a tab for about half a second
  shows a card under it: the full name, the path, the unsaved state and, for
  another tab, the note from where it was left (an excerpt of about 3,000
  characters cut at top-level blocks, rendered at a small native text size).
  Moving along the tabs follows at once; a press, scroll, key or leaving the
  tab bar hides it. It never switches tabs or moves a reading position. It
  replaces the label's native tooltip.
- **Mixed lists.** In lists that mix tasks with plain or numbered items, a
  task's checkbox sits where the bullet goes, so text lines up at every
  depth, on screen and in exports. Reproduced first: task text sat about one
  checkbox width to the right of its neighbours.
- **Direct editing.** A random-edit fuzzer (`tests/ui/direct-edit-fuzz.spec.js`)
  types, deletes, pastes and formats at random places and checks the source
  after every step. The bugs it found are fixed in `direct-editing.js`,
  `composing.js` and `selection-tools.js`, each with a regression case.

Only renderer files changed since v0.4.0 (`src/`); the main process,
preload, installer and Electron are unchanged.
